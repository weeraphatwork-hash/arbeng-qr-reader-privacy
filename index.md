# ArBeng QR Reader — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 28, 2026  
**Publisher:** ArBeng  
**Privacy and support contact:** arbengqr.support@gmail.com

## About the extension

ArBeng QR Reader is a Chrome extension that helps users scan QR codes in a visible browser tab, a region selected on a webpage, or an image or screenshot they explicitly upload or paste. It also creates static QR codes from text or web addresses. The user initiates these operations.

## Information processed

When a user uses one of these features, the extension may temporarily process visible tab images, selected or pasted screenshots, decoded QR code content, and text or URLs entered to generate a QR code. Depending on the user's selection, this content may contain personal or sensitive information.

## Purpose and local processing

This information is processed to perform QR code scanning, decoding, display, generation, copying, downloading or user-requested opening of a decoded URL. In the reviewed v0.1.1.10 extension build, QR processing runs locally inside the browser. The reviewed package does not contain a publisher-operated backend, advertising or analytics SDK, or code that uploads screenshots, decoded QR contents, or QR generator inputs to a publisher-operated server.

ArBeng does not sell the information processed by the extension or use it for personalized advertising or profiling.

## Sharing and external services

The reviewed extension does not transmit scanned screenshots, decoded QR text or user-entered QR generator inputs to ArBeng servers or advertising services. When the user chooses to open a decoded URL, Chrome navigates to the destination. That destination website may receive information through normal browser navigation and is governed by its own privacy practices.

If the user copies or downloads a QR code or its contents, the clipboard, downloaded file, browser history, destination application or operating system may retain it under the user's own settings.

## Storage and retention

In the reviewed build, images and decoded QR data are held temporarily in browser memory for the requested action; no publisher-controlled database or persistent Chrome storage is used to retain scanned content. Information that the user deliberately downloads, copies or opens may persist in the destination application, clipboard, browser history or local files.

## Browser permissions

- **activeTab:** Provides temporary access to the current active tab after a user-initiated QR scan, including access needed to analyze visible tab content.
- **scripting:** Allows the extension to insert on-page QR selection and result interfaces on supported webpages after a user request.

The reviewed build requests no persistent host permissions for all websites. Chrome-restricted pages may not support direct on-page scanning; images and screenshots can be scanned separately.

## User choices and security

QR code scans and QR generation are initiated by the user. The extension does not automatically navigate to a decoded destination; the user must choose to open it. QR codes can contain harmful or deceptive links, so users should review the destination before opening it.

## Limited Use

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is used only as needed to provide the QR scanning and generation features described above.

## Updates and contact

This policy will be updated if information-handling practices change. For privacy or support questions, contact **arbengqr.support@gmail.com**.
